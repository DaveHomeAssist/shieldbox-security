The ShieldBox Standard

What You Get With Every Event
No temp agency staffing.
No shortcuts. No surprises.
Owner on site, every event. Carl Mercer leads from the floor. No delegate management, no subcontracted strangers.
Trained, in-house team. Every ShieldBox guard is vetted, trained, and familiar with our operational standards before setting foot on your event.
Clear chain of command. Every post knows their role, their escalation path, and their supervisor before doors open.
Direct coordination with venue staff. We work with your production team and venue contacts before the event, not during a problem.
Proposal returned within 24 hours. You receive a detailed, itemized quote — not a template with your name swapped in.

ShieldBox Security is not a mass staffing agency. We are a small, specialized team that takes on a limited number of events each month. That means your event gets our full attention — from initial intake through post-event debrief. Every client gets Carl's personal number. Every event gets a pre-site walkthrough when possible.
