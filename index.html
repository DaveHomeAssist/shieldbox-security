What We Do

Security Services

Event coverage is the core offer: doors, floor, perimeter, and everything between load-in and load-out across Philadelphia.

Crowd Management

High-density monitoring, queue management, and de-escalation by trained floor personnel.
Best for: concerts, festivals, and events with 200+ attendees

Talent Protection

Dedicated close-protection escorts for performers, VIP guests, and principals on site.
Best for: performers, VIPs, and high-profile guests requiring close-protection detail

Front of House

Entry screening, bag checks, and visible security presence managing first impressions.
Best for: venue entrances, galas, and private events with guest lists or credential checks

Perimeter Patrol

Active roaming patrols securing venue exterior and adjacent loading areas throughout the event.
Best for: outdoor venues, multi-entrance sites, and events with loading dock access

Overnight Watch

Stationary overnight guard for venue equipment, staging, and production assets after hours.
Best for: multi-day events, festivals, and venues with overnight equipment or staging
